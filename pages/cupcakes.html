VANILLA CUPCAKES

Ingredients

  • 1 ⅔ cup all-purpose fluor (213 g)
  • 1 cup granulated sugar (200 g)
  • ¼ tsp baking soda
  • 1 ½ tsp baking powder
  • 3 egg whites or 2 whole eggs
  • 1 tbsp vanille extract (15 ml)
  • ½ cup sour cream (120 ml)
  • ½ cup whole milk warm (120 ml)

Preparation

Preheat oven to 177 C. Place cupcake papers in a cupcake tin.

In a bowl, whisk the dry ingredients together (fluor, sugar, baking soda and baking powder) until well combined and set aside.

In a separate bowl, add and whisk the sour cream, milk, vanilla, melted butter and eggs.

Add the wet mixture to the dry and whisk together until smooth.

Scoop the batter into the cupcake papers and fill about ⅔ the way up and bake to 350 F (176 C) for about 18 minutes or until the centers just spring to the touch.
